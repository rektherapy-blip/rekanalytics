Pay your clinicians from an export, not a spreadsheet.

Behavioral health agencies that pay contractors a share of net collections often lose days every pay period to matching payments, applying each clinician's rate, and chasing recoupments.

We build a repeatable process around your EHR export, such as TherapyNotes or SimplePractice. It applies the right rate, accounts for adjustments and recoupments, keeps an audit trail, and produces a statement for every clinician. Your billing lead reviews and approves. Nothing is paid until a person signs off.

Talk about your payroll process

  1. Export

    Your billing team exports payments from the EHR, the same way they do today.

  2. Apply the rules

    Rates, adjustments, and recoupments are calculated consistently and logged for audit.

  3. Review and pay

    A statement for each clinician is ready to check, approve, and send to your payment system.
