From Solo Practice to Consulting Business: How Kay Arispe, LICSW Built Sage Therapy

ByREK Analytics
CategoryClient Feature
Read time7 min
Live sitesagetherapyne.com

There is a version of this story that sounds straightforward: a licensed clinical social worker in Blair, Nebraska builds a private practice, finds success, and expands into consulting. Clean arc, tidy ending.

The real version is more interesting. Kay Arispe, LICSW, PMH-C built her private practice the hard way, self-taught, without a roadmap, figuring out the business side of clinical practice largely on her own while simultaneously doing the clinical work she trained for. And it was precisely that experience, with all its uncertainty and trial and error, that eventually became the most valuable thing she had to offer other clinicians.

Today, Sage Therapy and Consulting operates as two distinct but complementary practices under one roof: a growing clinical therapy practice serving clients across Nebraska and Iowa, and a consulting practice that helps other therapists build private practices of their own. REK Analytics is proud to be part of the infrastructure supporting that growth.

The Practice That Demand Built

Kay did not set out to become a practice-building consultant. She set out to be a therapist. But when you build something from scratch in a field where the business side receives almost no formal training, and then you do it well enough that other clinicians start asking how you did it, the consulting work has a way of finding you before you go looking for it.

That is exactly what happened. As Kay's clinical reputation grew in the Blair area and beyond, fellow therapists began reaching out. How did you get credentialed? How do you manage intake? What EHR do you use and how do you set it up? How did you set your fees? How do you actually get clients through the door?

These are not questions that graduate programs in clinical social work spend much time on. Programs train clinicians to do clinical work. They do not typically train clinicians to build, operate, and grow a business, which is what private practice ownership actually requires.

The Gap in Clinical Training

Most therapists enter private practice having received extensive clinical training and very little business education. Insurance credentialing, EHR setup, billing workflows, fee-setting, intake systems, and practice policies are all learned through experience, or not learned at all, which is where many practices struggle. This is the gap Kay Arispe identified and built a consulting practice around.

Kay's response to the demand was characteristically practical. Rather than pointing people toward generic resources or offering a blanket program that treats every clinician's situation the same, she formalized what she was already doing informally, sitting down with individual clinicians, learning where they were in the process, and helping them work through exactly what they were stuck on.

What Makes the Consulting Work

The consulting side of Sage Therapy and Consulting is deliberately individualized. Kay does not offer a one-size-fits-all course or a templated program. She offers one-on-one consulting sessions structured around where each clinician actually is in the process of building or growing their practice.

The range of what consulting covers reflects the real complexity of private practice ownership:

  • Insurance credentialing and payer enrollment, one of the most confusing and time-consuming parts of launching a practice
  • CAQH and NPI setup, the credentialing infrastructure that most new clinicians have never encountered before
  • EHR selection and SimplePractice workflow setup, getting the right system in place and actually knowing how to use it
  • Billing and claims processes, understanding how money actually moves through a therapy practice
  • Intake and documentation systems, the infrastructure that keeps a practice running efficiently
  • Practice policies and procedures, the framework that protects both the clinician and the client
  • Fee-setting and financial planning, approaching practice finances with clarity rather than guesswork
  • Expansion planning, adding clinicians, preparing for a group practice model, scaling thoughtfully

The consulting engagements range from a focused single session for clinicians still in the contemplative stage to comprehensive multi-session packages for those ready to work through the full launch process in depth. Visit sagetherapyne.com for current consulting offerings and availability.

A Practice That Is Actively Growing

The consulting work is only part of the story. The clinical practice at Sage Therapy and Consulting is expanding in its own right, and doing so in a way that reflects both the demand for Kay's services and the deliberate way she approaches growth.

Sage Therapy and Consulting now serves clients across Nebraska and Iowa, offering in-person sessions at the Blair, Nebraska office and telehealth to clients throughout both states. The clinical specialties are deep and specific, anxiety, depression, trauma, ADHD, burnout, life transitions, relationship concerns, and perinatal mental health, for which Kay holds the PMH-C (Perinatal Mental Health Certified) credential through Postpartum Support International.

The PMH-C credential is not a minor addition. It reflects specialized, evidence-based training in one of the most clinically nuanced areas of mental health: the period spanning pregnancy, postpartum, and the broader transition to parenthood. For clients navigating that season of life, it represents a meaningful level of focused expertise that is far from universal among therapists.

Expanding Services

Sage Therapy and Consulting is actively expanding its clinical capacity. Services for adolescent clients ages 13 and older are coming soon, broadening the practice's reach to younger clients and their families. Kay is also beginning EMDR training in October 2026, with plans to offer this evidence-based trauma therapy modality after completing training requirements. The practice is building toward a group practice model: bringing on additional clinicians who share the same commitment to thoughtful, approachable, high-quality care.

The insurance panel reflects the breadth of the client base Kay serves: BCBS, Medicare, Nebraska Medicaid, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, CHAMPVA, Medica, Lyra Health, and Midlands Choice. For a practice located in Blair, a smaller Nebraska communityand this accessibility matters. It means that geography and insurance coverage are not barriers to quality clinical care for the clients who need it.

I built my private practice from the ground up, learning the business side along the way, and that experience eventually became the foundation for the consulting work I now offer to other clinicians.

Kay Arispe, LICSW, PMH-C, Sage Therapy & Consulting

What REK Analytics Is Building With Sage

REK Analytics partnered with Sage Therapy and Consulting to design and launch the practice's digital presence, a full-service engagement that went well beyond putting a website online.

The website at sagetherapyne.com was built from the ground up to serve two distinct audiences simultaneously: prospective therapy clients looking for mental health support, and clinicians exploring consulting services. Getting that architecture right required understanding both audiences, their different motivations, and the different paths they take through the site.

As Sage expands toward a group practice model, the complexity of the business grows with it. Revenue tracking across multiple clinicians. Insurance payer performance. Session volume trends. Why This Story Matters

The private practice consulting space is growing. As more therapists pursue independent practice ownership and discover how little their training prepared them for the business side, the demand for practical, individualized guidance from someone who has actually done it is only increasing.

What makes Kay Arispe's approach stand out in that growing field is the same thing that makes her clinical work effective: it is grounded in real experience, it is individualized rather than templated, and it does not pretend that the path to a successful private practice looks the same for every clinician.

Sage Therapy and Consulting is not a course. It is not a program. It is a working practice run by someone who built one from scratch, understands what that actually requires, and has enough respect for other clinicians to meet them where they are rather than selling them a formula.

REK Analytics is glad to be part of building the infrastructure that supports what Kay is doing, and we are looking forward to the next chapter as Sage Therapy and Consulting continues to grow.
