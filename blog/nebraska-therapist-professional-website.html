The Problem Every New Private Practice Clinician Faces

Launching a therapy private practice in Nebraska is not just a clinical undertaking. It is a business launch. And like any business, the first question a potential client asks is: can I find you online?

The average therapy client does not get a referral and immediately make a phone call. They Google. They compare websites. They read bios. They check insurance. They look for a way to request an appointment without picking up the phone. If your digital presence does not meet them where they are, they move to the next name on the list.

The Real Cost of No Website

A therapy practice with no web presence is effectively invisible to the majority of potential clients. Over 70% of people search online before choosing a healthcare provider. Without a website and Google Business Profile, a practice cannot be found, compared, or contacted by the clients who need it most.

The traditional solution is to hire a web design agency. In Omaha and across Nebraska, that typically means spending between $3,000 and $8,000, waiting six to twelve weeks, and then discovering that the agency built a good-looking site with no SEO, no Google Search Console setup, and no understanding of what HIPAA-compliant booking actually requires. We approached it differently.

Why HIPAA Compliance Cannot Be an Afterthought

This is where most DIY therapy websites fall short, and where the consequences are serious. HIPAA applies to every therapist in private practice. It governs how client information is collected, transmitted, and stored — including through your website.

A contact form that routes client information through a standard Gmail account is not HIPAA compliant. A booking system that stores appointment requests in an unencrypted database is not HIPAA compliant. A standard web form that asks about the reason for seeking therapy and routes that response through a third-party email service is not HIPAA compliant.

What HIPAA Compliance on a Therapy Website Actually Requires

Every tool that handles Protected Health Information must have a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with the practice. This includes booking platforms, contact forms, messaging tools, and email systems. SimplePractice signs a BAA with every provider and maintains HIPAA-compliant infrastructure for scheduling, messaging, telehealth, and intake. This is why we integrate SimplePractice as the exclusive booking and contact mechanism for every therapy site we build — not a custom form, not a generic widget.

For every therapy site REK Analytics builds, every client-facing interaction routes through SimplePractice. The booking button connects directly to the HIPAA-compliant client portal. The contact button opens the SimplePractice secure contact form. No Protected Health Information touches an unsecured system at any point in the flow.
