The Problem Every New Private Practice Therapist Faces

Launching a therapy private practice in Nebraska is not just a clinical undertaking. It is a business launch. And like any business, the first question a potential client asks is: can I find you online?

The average therapy client does not get a referral and immediately make a phone call. They Google. They compare websites. They read bios. They check insurance. They look for a way to request an appointment without picking up the phone. If your digital presence does not meet them where they are, they move to the next name on the list.

For Tracy, the stakes were clear. She was accepting both private pay and Blue Cross Blue Shield of Nebraska clients. She was offering telehealth services statewide. She had real clinical expertise and a specialty that clients in Nebraska were actively searching for. But without a website, none of that was discoverable.

The Real Cost of No Website

A therapy practice with no web presence is effectively invisible to the majority of potential clients. Over 70% of people search online before choosing a healthcare provider. Without a website and Google Business Profile, a practice cannot be found, compared, or contacted by the clients who need it most.

The traditional solution is to hire a web design agency. In Omaha and across Nebraska, that typically means spending between $3,000 and $8,000, waiting six to twelve weeks, and then discovering that the agency built a good-looking site with no SEO, no Google Search Console setup, and no understanding of what HIPAA-compliant booking actually requires.

Why HIPAA Compliance Cannot Be an Afterthought

This is where most DIY therapy websites fall short, and where the consequences are serious. HIPAA applies to every therapist in private practice. It governs how client information is collected, transmitted, and stored — including through your website.

A contact form that routes client information through a standard Gmail account is not HIPAA compliant. A booking system that stores appointment requests in an unencrypted database is not HIPAA compliant. A standard web form that asks about the reason for seeking therapy and routes that response through a third-party email service is not HIPAA compliant.

What HIPAA Compliance on a Therapy Website Actually Requires

Every tool that handles Protected Health Information must have a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with the practice. This includes booking platforms, contact forms, messaging tools, and email systems. SimplePractice signs a BAA with every provider and maintains HIPAA-compliant infrastructure for scheduling, messaging, telehealth, and intake. This is why we integrated SimplePractice as the exclusive booking and contact mechanism for REK Therapy — not a custom form, not a generic widget.

For Tracy's site, every client-facing interaction routes through SimplePractice. The booking button connects directly to her HIPAA-compliant client portal. The contact button opens her SimplePractice secure contact form. No Protected Health Information touches an unsecured system at any point in the flow.

Full SEO Implementation

We implemented a complete on-page SEO package including keyword-optimized title tags and meta descriptions targeting high-intent therapy searches in Nebraska, Schema.org structured data markup identifying the practice as a MedicalBusiness with named services and credentials, Open Graph and Twitter Card meta tags for social sharing, canonical URL configuration, geographic targeting meta tags for Nebraska-specific results, and sitemap submission to Google Search Console.

Google Business Profile Setup

We guided Tracy through the complete Google Business Profile process — category selection, service area configuration for telehealth, a business description optimized for local search, and phone verification. The profile was live and appearing in Google Maps results within days of launch. For a telehealth practice, appearing in Google's local knowledge panel is one of the highest-impact visibility moves available at zero ongoing cost.

Fast, free hosting through Vercel connected to a GitHub repository for version-controlled deployments. Domain purchased through Namecheap, DNS configured, SSL certificate issued automatically. The site loads in under one second. Hosting costs nothing on an ongoing basis beyond the annual domain registration fee.

SimplePractice Integration

Rather than build a custom booking form that creates HIPAA liability, we integrated Tracy's existing SimplePractice account as the sole booking and contact mechanism on the site. The booking button routes directly to her HIPAA-compliant client portal. The contact button opens her SimplePractice secure contact form. Clean, compliant, and already familiar to her existing workflow.

What Therapists in Nebraska Should Know

Whether you work with REK Analytics or not, here is what every therapist opening a private practice in Nebraska needs to understand about their digital presence:

  • Your website is the first thing a potential client sees. It needs to communicate trust and show a clear next step within the first ten seconds
  • HIPAA compliance on your website is not optional — every tool that touches client information needs a signed BAA
  • SimplePractice, TherapyNotes, and a handful of other EHR platforms are purpose-built for this — use them for booking and client communication rather than building custom forms
  • Google Business Profile is free and generates significant local search visibility for practices serving Nebraska clients
  • SEO is not a one-time task — structured data, keyword-optimized content, and Google Search Console verification are baseline requirements for a site that ranks
